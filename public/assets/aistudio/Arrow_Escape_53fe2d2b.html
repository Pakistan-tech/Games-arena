How to play, and why it trains your brain

Tap an arrow to send it off the board. If another arrow is in the way, you lose a life and the blocker flashes red. Depth is how many rounds a perfect solver needs: higher means longer chains of “this blocks that, which blocks the next.”

  • Planning ahead: trace each arrow’s path and the chain behind it before you tap.
  • Working memory: hold several dependencies in mind at once.
  • Spatial reasoning: follow bending bodies and straight paths across a large grid.
  • Impulse control: lives make guessing costly, so careful checking pays off.

Every puzzle is generated so it can always be solved, and the game re-checks that with a solver before showing it.
